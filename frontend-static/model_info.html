What is Indra OCR?

Indra OCR is a 1M-parameter OCR model that reads up to 49 pages per second on CPU. It turns PDFs and images into structured Markdown, plain text, or JSON with bounding boxes, layout classifications, and reading order.

  • Throughput: 49 pages/s, 11–17× faster than Tesseract and PaddleOCR on the same machine.
  • Latency: ~230 ms per page on 4 x86 vCPUs, 12–24× faster than alternatives.
  • Accuracy: Scores 62.1 on OmniParseBench headline, beating PaddleOCR's full PP-StructureV3 pipeline (58.4).
  • Layout & Boxes: Bounding box for every line and layout block with semantic labels and reading order.
  • Digital PDFs: Reads the PDF's text layer in any script (75+ language families) and OCRs only what is missing.
  • Ultra Lightweight: ~18 MB total ONNX Runtime models, completely free of PyTorch.

Language Support

  • OCR for Scans & Images: Latin (50+ languages), Cyrillic, Greek, Devanagari, Bengali, and Chinese (simplified and traditional).
  • Text Layer (Digital PDFs): Any script. Verified on 75 language families including Arabic, Hebrew, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, and Southeast Asian scripts.

Layout Block Labels

The layout model segments pages into structural blocks in reading order:

  • Text: Standard body paragraphs.
  • Section-Header: Document headings and titles.
  • Page-Header & Page-Footer: Running margins and page numbers (optionally stripped unless furniture is kept).
  • List-Group: Bulleted and numbered lists.
  • Table: Grid tables rendered as HTML tables.
  • Caption: Captions under figures and tables.
  • Footnote: Bottom-of-page references.
  • Table-Of-Contents: Outlines and indices.
  • Form: Form fields and key-value inputs.
